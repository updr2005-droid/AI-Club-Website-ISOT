The AI Club at IFIM College is a student-led initiative dedicated to fostering interest and expertise in Artificial Intelligence and related technologies. Our mission is to create a collaborative environment where students can learn, experiment, and innovate.

We organize workshops, hackathons, guest lectures, and research projects to provide hands-on experience with AI technologies. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced coder, our club offers opportunities for everyone to grow.
