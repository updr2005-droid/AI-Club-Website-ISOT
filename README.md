# 🤖 AI Club — IFIM School of Technology

Welcome to the official **AI Club Website of IFIM School of Technology, Bengaluru**.

The AI Club is a student-driven community focused on **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Agentic AI, Automation, and emerging technologies**. The club provides students with opportunities to learn, build, experiment, collaborate, and showcase innovative technology projects.

> **Learn • Build • Innovate**

---

## 🌐 About the Website

This website serves as the digital hub of the AI Club, providing information about:

* 🤖 AI Club initiatives and activities
* 🚀 Projects and technical experiments
* 📅 Workshops, events, and sessions
* 🏆 Hackathons and competitions
* 👨‍💻 Student achievements
* 📚 Learning resources
* 👥 AI Club team and leadership
* 📢 Club announcements and updates

The website is designed to provide students with a simple and engaging way to discover AI-related opportunities and participate in club activities.

## How the Workflow Works

### 1. Discover

Students visit the AI Club website to discover upcoming events, technical activities, projects and learning opportunities.

### 2. Explore

Students explore different areas of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Agentic AI, automation and other emerging technologies.

### 3. Participate

Students participate in workshops, technical sessions, competitions, hackathons and club activities.

### 4. Build

Students collaborate with other members to develop practical projects and experiment with new technologies.

### 5. Showcase

Completed projects, achievements and activities can be showcased through the AI Club website.

### 6. Innovate

The final goal is to encourage students to transform their ideas into innovative and practical solutions.
